Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers the paid Kepful plan, Pro. The free plan costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund. It is part of our Terms of Service.

In short: cancel any time in Kepful. If you are not happy, ask within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full.

Cancel any time

In Kepful, open Settings, then Plan and billing, then Manage subscription. That opens Paddle's billing page, where you can cancel.

  • You keep Pro until the end of the period you have already paid for, and you are not charged again.
  • After that your account moves to the free plan. Your clients, notes and history stay yours, on any plan.
  • If you cannot get into Kepful, email us from your account email and we will cancel it for you.

14-day refund

When you start a new Pro subscription, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your first payment. This applies to the monthly, yearly and founding plans. You do not have to explain why, and having used Kepful during those 14 days does not stop you from getting your money back.

  • The 14 days start on the day of your first payment for that subscription.
  • Later renewal payments are not covered by the 14-day refund, except where the law says otherwise. To avoid a renewal charge, cancel before the renewal date. You can cancel any time from Settings → Plan and billing in the extension.

Receipts and renewal reminders

Paddle sends you a receipt for every payment, and a reminder before a yearly renewal where the law requires it. You can cancel any time from Settings → Plan and billing in the extension.

How to ask for a refund

Email us at support@kepful.app from the email address you use for Kepful, and say you would like a refund. If you have it, include the receipt number from Paddle, though it is not required. We will confirm by email once it has been processed.

What happens after a refund

  • The refunded subscription ends straight away and your account moves to the free plan. Your notes and history stay in your account.
  • If you had the founding price, it ends with the subscription. If you subscribe again later, the standard price applies, and your founding place goes back to the pool for someone else.

How you get your money back

Paddle is the seller of record for your purchase, so refunds are made by Paddle to the original payment method, and the refund will show as coming from Paddle. It can take several business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer.

Chargebacks and disputes

Please email us before disputing a payment with your bank, because we can usually fix it faster. If a payment is charged back, we end that subscription immediately, and we may not be able to offer you a new one.

Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under the law where you live.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, write to support@kepful.app.
