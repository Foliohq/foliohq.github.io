Kepful Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Kepful is a Chrome extension that helps you remember every client, call by call. It records the audio of a browser tab you choose, turns it into notes, summaries and action items, and lets you ask questions about your clients. No bot ever joins your call. This policy explains what Kepful collects, why, and what control you have.

In short: we use your data to give you your summaries, client notes and answers. We never sell it, we never use it for advertising, and our team does not read it unless you ask us for support.

What Kepful collects

  • Call audio. Only when you press Start Recording, Kepful captures the audio of the tab you chose. Kepful does not record anything otherwise, and it does not keep the audio recording after it has been transcribed.
  • Content you create. Transcripts, summaries, action items, client names and profiles, and the questions you ask in Ask Kepful, Kepful's assistant.
  • Email address. You sign in to Kepful with your email address and a one-time 6-digit code we send to it. We use your email only to let you sign in, to keep your data tied to your account, and to contact you about support if you ask. We do not send marketing email.
  • Account identifier. Kepful creates a private account identifier so your data stays separate from everyone else's.
  • Usage records. A simple record of each call sent for transcription and each AI request: the date, the feature, how long the audio was or how much text was processed, your plan, and an estimated cost. They contain none of your call content. We use them to apply plan limits and fair use, and to understand what the service costs to run.
  • Subscription details. If you subscribe to Pro, we store a customer ID and subscription ID from our payment provider, your plan, its status, the date it renews or ends, and whether you have the founding price. We do not receive or store your card number or other payment details.
  • Local settings. Your consent choice and preferences are stored in your browser by Chrome's extension storage.

How your data is used

Kepful uses your data to provide its features: transcribing your calls, writing summaries and action items, keeping your client profiles up to date, and answering your questions. Kepful does not use your data for advertising or profiling. See Service providers below for how third parties handle it.

Service providers

To provide transcription, summaries and answers, we send your call audio and text to trusted third-party service providers for speech-to-text and AI language processing. They process this data on our behalf to provide Kepful. Some providers may use submitted content to improve their own services, subject to their terms and to any opt-out we have enabled. We do not sell your data. A list of our providers is available on request at support@kepful.app.

We also use a cloud database provider, which stores your account data, clients, meetings and tasks in a protected database where each user can reach only their own data, and Paddle as the merchant of record for Pro payments (see Payments below). Requests to our providers pass through Kepful's own secure service, so no provider keys are stored in your browser.

Payments

Pro payments are handled by Paddle, which is the merchant of record for your purchase. When you pay, you give your payment details, name, email address and billing country to Paddle, not to us, so that it can take the payment, work out tax, send your receipt and prevent fraud. Paddle does this under its own privacy notice. Paddle tells us the subscription details listed above, and nothing more about your payment. Invoices and tax records are kept by Paddle, as the law requires.

This website

Our website uses no cookies and no trackers, and its fonts and images come from us. Two things are worth knowing. The pricing section asks our own server how many founding places are left, and sends no personal information. And the checkout page, kepful.app/upgrade, loads Paddle's checkout script from Paddle, which may set cookies and collect technical details such as your IP address and device so that it can process your payment. That page is the only place on our site that loads anything from a third party.

Optional features you control

  • Notion. If you choose to export to Notion, Kepful sends the content you export to Notion using your own connection. This happens only when you ask for it.
  • Email drafts. Kepful can open a draft email in Gmail. It only prepares the draft; Kepful never sends email for you.
  • Copy and PDF. These happen on your device.

Who can see your data

Our team does not read your calls, transcripts or client notes. We may access specific data only if you ask us for support and agree to it, if it is needed to keep the service secure or fix abuse, or if the law requires it. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs and Chrome extension APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Kepful does not transfer your data to third parties except to provide or improve the features you use, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or sale of the business with notice to you. It does not use your data for advertising or for judging creditworthiness, and humans do not read it except in the narrow cases described above.

Recording and other people

Kepful records the tab you choose, which may include other people on your call. Please make sure everyone has agreed to be recorded, as laws on recording vary by place. You are responsible for getting that consent.

Retention and deletion

Where your data is kept, and for how long:

  • Your account (our database). Your clients, call transcripts, summaries, action items, client profiles and settings stay until you delete them. You can delete one call or one client at any time in the Kepful dashboard; that permanently removes its transcript, notes, tasks and profile. Signing out does not delete anything.
  • Delete all my data. In Settings, under Your data, the “Delete all my data” button removes all of the above from our database and from your device in one step, and asks our speech-to-text provider to delete any transcript it still holds. It does not close your sign-in account or cancel a Pro subscription. You can also email us to ask for your account to be closed.
  • The call recording. The audio is kept on your device only until its transcript has come back, then deleted. If an upload fails, it stays on your device for up to 7 days so you can retry, then is deleted automatically.
  • Our speech-to-text provider. To make a transcript, the audio is sent to our speech-to-text provider. As soon as Kepful has saved the transcript to your account, it asks the provider to delete its copy. According to the provider's published terms at the time of writing, uploaded audio is removed within 24 to 48 hours of upload, and a transcript that has not been deleted is removed after 30 days.
  • Our AI provider. Transcripts and notes are sent to our AI provider to write summaries and answers. According to the provider's published terms at the time of writing, it deletes the requests and responses from its systems within 30 days.
  • Usage records. They contain no call content. They are kept while your account exists; if your account is closed, they are kept without your identity.
  • Subscription and payment records. Subscription details are kept while your account exists. Invoices and tax records are kept by Paddle and by us as the law requires, even after you delete your data.
  • On your device. Profile photos you add are resized and stored only on your device, never uploaded to our servers or shared with our service providers. Your preferences and chat history also stay on your device. All of it is removed by “Delete all my data”, and photos are also removed when you remove them or delete the client.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, stored in a protected database with per-user access rules, and AI provider keys are kept on our servers only. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Kepful is intended for professionals and is not directed at children under 16.

Earlier Pro waitlist

If you joined the Pro waitlist before Pro launched, we kept your email address only to tell you that Pro is available. Email support@kepful.app to be removed.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where needed, ask for your agreement again inside Kepful.

Contact

Questions, support, or deletion requests: support@kepful.app
