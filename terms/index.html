1. The service

7. Usage limits and fair use

Each plan has limits, and Kepful shows them to you too:

  • Free: up to 5 recorded calls a month, each up to 90 minutes, and up to 10 Ask Kepful questions a day.
  • Pro: up to 40 hours of recording a month, each call up to 2 hours, and Ask Kepful with no fixed daily limit, within fair use (below).
  • A call is counted when its audio is sent for transcription, and recording time is counted by the real length of the audio. If a transcription fails, the call is not counted against you.
  • Monthly limits reset at 00:00 UTC on the 1st of each month (5:30 AM on the 1st in India). Daily limits reset at 00:00 UTC every day. Kepful shows reset times in your own time zone.
  • If you reach a limit, Kepful tells you. A call that is too long for your plan stays on your device, so you can upgrade and retry it.

Fair use. “Unlimited” Ask Kepful means unlimited for normal professional use by you. To keep Kepful available for everyone, we apply daily safety limits (currently about 100 questions a day on Pro, and a cap on how many calls you can send in one day) and overall capacity limits across all users, so at busy times you may be asked to try again later. Please do not share one account between several people, resell Kepful, send requests with scripts or bots, or try to get around a limit. If an account does, we may slow, limit or suspend it, and where it is reasonable we will tell you first. We may change limits. If a change would meaningfully reduce what you get on a paid plan, we will tell you in advance.
