Questions, answered

Does a bot join my calls?

No. Kepful records the audio of the browser tab you choose, directly in Chrome. Nothing joins the call and no one receives an invite.

Which call apps work?

Any call that runs in a Chrome tab, such as Google Meet, Zoom or Microsoft Teams in the browser. If a call happened in a desktop app, you can upload the recording instead.

Do my clients need to know?

Yes. Always ask everyone on the call for permission before recording. Kepful reminds you, and laws on recording differ by place.

Who can see my data?

Only you can see it in Kepful. It is stored under your account, never sold, never used for advertising, and our team does not read it unless you ask for support. To make transcripts and summaries we send it to service providers who process it for us; see the Privacy Policy.

What happens when I reach my limit?

Before you press Start, Kepful tells you if you have no recorded calls left. Monthly limits reset at 00:00 UTC on the 1st of the month, and Ask Kepful’s daily limit resets at 00:00 UTC (5:30 AM India time). If a call runs past your plan’s per-call limit (90 minutes on Free, 2 hours on Pro), Kepful warns you while you record and keeps the audio on your device, so you can upgrade and retry it.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Open Kepful in Chrome and click Upgrade, in the dashboard sidebar or in Settings. Checkout starts from inside Kepful, so your purchase is linked to your account. Payments are handled by Paddle, our payment partner and merchant of record, so we never see your card details.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, any time: Settings, then Plan and billing, then Manage subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, and your notes and history stay yours on any plan. Paddle sends you a receipt for every payment, and a reminder before a yearly renewal where the law requires it. Within 14 days of your first payment you can also ask for a full refund.

Does Kepful email my clients?

Never. It can prepare a Gmail draft, but you review it and send it yourself.
